# Data source notes
- OpenCity BMTC Bus Stops and Routes: https://data.opencity.in/dataset/bengaluru-bus-stops-and-routes
- TUMI GTFS Bengaluru: https://hub.tumidata.org/dataset/gtfs-bengaluru (historical feed; check freshness and licence)
- OpenCity BMTC datasets: https://data.opencity.in/dataset/?organization=bengaluru-metropolitan-transport-corporation
- Hadoop Streaming documentation: https://hadoop.apache.org/docs/stable/hadoop-streaming/HadoopStreaming.html

Verify licensing and update dates before redistributing source datasets. No third-party dataset is bundled in this ZIP. Synthetic demonstration data is bundled instead.
